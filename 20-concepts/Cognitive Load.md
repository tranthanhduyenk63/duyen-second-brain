# Cognitive Load

> [!todo] Drafted by Claude: generic definition. Rewrite in your own words.

## Definition (in my words)
The amount of information and context a person must hold in working memory to do a task. When it is too high, quality and speed drop.

## Why it matters
- Mixed architectures, many repos, multiple roles and close supervision of agents all add to it.
- It's one of the main limits on how much one engineer can own.

## How to reduce
- A consistent architecture, delegating (to people or agents), smaller scopes, and good docs.

## When to use (this lens)
- When work feels slow or exhausting for reasons other than how hard the problem is.
